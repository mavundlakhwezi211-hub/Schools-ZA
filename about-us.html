About UrSKOOL

Built in Hazyview, Mpumalanga

Hey, I'm Khwezi Mavundla from Hazyview Comprehensive School in Mpumalanga.

I started building UrSKOOL in 2025. I love school and technology, so I decided to combine them and build something useful for my school.

UrSKOOL brings practical school tools and updates together, so learners, families, teachers, and school teams can find important information in one place.

This project started as an idea after class and turned into something real. I'm still a learner, still learning every day, and proud of what UrSKOOL is becoming.

Thank you for being part of the journey.

Tools for the school community

UrSKOOL supports everyday school work with features such as:

  • Attendance register: Record and review learner attendance digitally by date and class.
  • School timetables: Keep class schedules available in one place, with timetable files supported.
  • Announcements and posts: Share school updates, photos, and polls with the community.
  • Learner and parent information: Keep learner details organized and help parents stay connected with their children’s school information.

More time for teaching and learning

When schedules, attendance, and updates are available digitally, staff can spend less time preparing and redistributing paper notices. Learners and parents can check shared information when they need it, helping reduce avoidable follow-ups and keep the school community in sync.

Less paper and ink

Sharing announcements and timetables digitally can reduce the number of copies schools need to print. Recording attendance digitally can also reduce reliance on printed registers. The actual paper and ink saved depends on how many school processes move online and which documents still need to be printed.
