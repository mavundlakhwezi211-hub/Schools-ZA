About UrSKOOL

Built in Hazyview, Mpumalanga

Hey, I'm Khwezi Mavundla from Hazyview Comprehensive School in Mpumalanga.

I started building UrSKOOL in 2025.

I love school. I love tech. So I decided to combine them and build something useful for my school.

UrSKOOL is a platform where learners can upload homework, check marks, get study materials, and stay updated — all from their phone. Teachers can manage classes easier. Parents can see what's happening without waiting for a meeting.

It's simple, it's fast, and it was built right here in Hazyview.

This project started as an idea after class and turned into something real. I'm still a learner, still learning every day, but I'm proud of what UrSKOOL is becoming.

Thank you for being part of the journey.

— Khwezi

Founder, UrSKOOL | Hazyview Comprehensive School | Est. 2025
