1. What information do we collect?

In short: we collect information you give us and some information your device shares automatically.

Personal information you disclose to us. When you create a profile on UrSKOOL we collect: full name, email address, role (learner, teacher, parent, administrator), school name, grade or class, province, and your profile picture if you upload one. When you use the app we also store the content you create: posts, announcements, homework uploads, marks, attendance and timetable entries, and messages you send through the inbox.

Information collected automatically. Like most apps, our service stores small preferences on your own device (localStorage): your theme choice, class-format choice and cookie consent. We do not run advertising trackers.

2. How do we process your information?

In short: to run your school account, show school content, remember preferences, keep the app safe and comply with the law.

  • To create and manage your account and connect you with your school.
  • To display homework, marks, study material, announcements, timetables and messages.
  • To remember your theme and class-format preferences.
  • To protect the platform, fix problems and comply with legal duties.

3. Legal bases (POPIA)

South Africa's Protection of Personal Information Act (POPIA) requires a lawful basis. We rely on your consent, on providing the service you asked for, and on our legitimate interest in running a safe school platform. We only collect what is adequate and relevant.

4. When and with whom do we share?

In short: we do not sell your data. Information is visible the way the app is designed to work.

  • Teachers see learners in their schools and classes; parents see their children's updates.
  • We share only when required by law or to protect safety.

5. How long do we keep it?

Device preferences stay on your device until you clear them. Account and school data is kept while your account is active. You may ask us to delete it at any time at hello@urskool.co.za.

6. How do we keep it safe?

We use reasonable organisational and technical safeguards. No online service is 100% secure, so please keep your device and login details safe.

7. Your privacy rights

  • Request access to, correction or deletion of your information.
  • Withdraw consent or object to processing where POPIA allows.
  • Complain to the Information Regulator of South Africa.

8. Children's privacy

UrSKOOL is used by school learners. Learners under 18 should use the app with a parent, guardian or teacher's permission and supervision.

9. Cookies

We use only essential on-device storage (consent, theme, sign-in). No advertising cookies. The cookie banner appears once on the homepage and remembers your choice.

10. Updates to this notice

We may update this notice and will change the date above. Please review it from time to time.
